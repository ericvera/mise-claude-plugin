# mise v3 keep/cut scorecard

Decision rule: cut by default (`decisions.md`). A mechanism stays only where run evidence
supports it, or where it guards against irreversible harm. Every verdict cites `E-###` rows in
`evidence.jsonl`; `R-*` ids are UNVERIFIED desk research from `../research/` and appear only as
supporting context, never as the sole basis for a verdict.

**Coverage.** All 790 inventory rows (365 MF + 425 MR) map to exactly one of the 68 mechanisms
below; the mapping is `mechanism-map.jsonl` and is script-checked (0 unmapped, 0 duplicated, 0
extra). Two further rows (`X1`, `X2`) are cross-cutting: their lines are counted inside the
mechanisms above, so they own no map rows.

**Prose lines** is the distinct non-blank lines the mechanism's rows cover. Per-mechanism sums
reach 761 shipped lines against 743 distinct, because 18 lines carry rows in two mechanisms.
`state.ts` is counted as code, not prose.

## Notes on three verdicts that need their numbers stated once more

**`M38` critic gate — cap at 3 rounds.** The bound is chosen from two independent
measurements. Token economics (E-012, E-013): a change costs 295k tokens at requirements
rounds 1-2 and 850k at rounds 3+; 1.20M at plan rounds 1-2 and 2.17M at 3+; rounds 6+ cost 3.2M
each. Convergence (E-015): of 60 recorded (run, gate) pairs, 38 settle at 3 rounds or fewer with
6 stalls, while the 22 that run longer contain 17 of the 23 stalls. A hard cap of 3 also deletes
the reason the stall machinery exists — recurrence classification, the three triggers, the 5/8
budgets and their resets are 14 of the mechanism's 32 lines and all of them only answer "when do
we stop", which the cap answers for free.

**`M52` documenter — cut.** This is the only mechanism in the corpus with measured cost, zero
measured catch, and measured evidence that it _causes_ the escape class it was added for. Cost:
166 spawns, 4.9% of output, 314.0M tokens, 15.7 agent-hours, rising to 10.0% of output in 2.1.0
(E-026, E-075). Catch: 0 findings, by design. Harm: documenter commits run an 11.66 comment:code
ratio against the implementer's 0.22 (E-027), one pass rewrote comments across 61 files (E-028),
and 2.1.0 exists to undo the regression its own release introduced (E-030). Prose returns to the
implementer, and the two lintable comment defects (D1 punctuation register, D4 length caps) move
to a ~20-line local ESLint rule over `sourceCode.getAllComments()` (E-044).

**`M18` project checklist — move-to-lint.** Named lint rules, with measured hit counts from the
read-only probe in `../runs/escapes.md` §1.4 (E-043): `no-restricted-syntax` on
`expect()` drilling (1,467 hits, ships as `warn` with a ratchet), on `mock.calls[<i>]` (974), on
an inline snapshot whose whole body is `undefined` (119) and on `toMatch(File)Snapshot` (0 hits — clean, so
it is free to turn on as an error); `vi.mock` with a factory or an internal path (70); a
path-scoped block erroring on any `hosting/components/atoms/**/*.test.ts`; `no-console` /
`no-debugger` for checklist rule 1; a `no-restricted-syntax` Literal scan for `REQ-` ids
(rule 3). Not lintable and therefore left as prose or dropped: coined jargon, stale comments,
missing rationale, history-log docs (E-044).
